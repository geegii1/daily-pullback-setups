How it works

The scanner runs each weekday against the S&P 500. A stock qualifies only if it passes all five rules.

The five rules

  1. Uptrend — price above the 50-day simple moving average
  2. Pullback entry — price within 3% above the 20-day low, or touching the 50-day average
  3. Liquidity — price over $20, average volume over 1M shares/day
  4. Volatility sanity — stop at 1.5× ATR below entry, under 8% of price
  5. Realistic target — entry + 1.5× risk, at or below the recent swing high

Judging outcomes

Each paper trade is judged after the close against the day's high/low: a high touching the target = win, a low breaching the stop = loss. If both are touched the same day, the stop is judged first and the trade is labeled ambiguous. Manual overrides are never overwritten.
